Who is Advance Pranjal?

Advance Pranjal is the personal technology platform and brand of Pranjal Kalita, a Computer Science postgraduate (M.Sc. Gauhati University) and robotics educator from Assam, India.

Driven by a deep passion for hardware experimentation and hands-on learning, Pranjal combines computer science fundamentals with physical electronics to demystify complex engineering concepts for students, beginners, and fellow makers across the globe.
